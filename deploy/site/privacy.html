Privacy Policy

Effective {{DATA}}.

This policy explains how {{EMPRESA}} ("we") handles information when sellers ("you") use {{MARCA}} (the "Service"), an operations assistant for marketplace sellers in the United States, Europe and Latin America. We act as a service provider (processor) for the data of your store and your buyers, and as a controller only for your own account data.

1. Information we process

  • Your account: operator names, password hashes (never the password), authenticator secrets (encrypted), sign-in times and the addresses of login attempts.
  • Marketplace authorization: the tokens and keys the marketplace issues when you connect your seller account, encrypted at rest. We never ask for or store your marketplace password.
  • Amazon data: order identifiers, status, dates, amounts, item SKUs, ASINs, titles and quantities, listings, prices and stock, read through the Selling Partner API. We do not request or store buyer names, addresses, e-mails or phone numbers from Amazon.
  • Mercado Livre data: orders, listings, buyer questions and, when you use the purchase flow, the buyer name, identifier and shipping address needed to fulfil the order. Buyer personal data is encrypted at rest with a key separate from the database.
  • Your configuration: products, suppliers, costs and business rules you enter.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the Service to you: synchronize and display your orders, listings and stock, calculate margins, check compliance rules, prepare actions for your approval (purchase orders, price changes, replies to buyers) and alert you about problems. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, do not combine data across clients and do not share Amazon or Mercado Livre data with anyone except as described in section 4.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Amazon information is deleted within 30 days after you revoke the authorization, after Amazon requests it, or when your account ends; restoring an older backup deletes it again.
  • Buyer personal data from Mercado Livre is purged automatically after the retention period configured for your instance (by default 1,825 days, or less if you choose), and earlier on a buyer's verified request.
  • Security logs are kept for at least 12 months and contain no buyer personal data.

4. Sharing and subprocessors

  • Hosting provider for the servers that run your isolated instance.
  • Anthropic, only if you enable automatic drafting of replies to Mercado Livre buyer questions: the question and the product sheet are sent to draft a reply that you review before it is published.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. Security

Each client runs in an isolated instance with its own database and encryption keys; traffic uses HTTPS; sign-in requires a password and an authenticator code; accounts lock after repeated failed attempts; credentials and buyer data are encrypted at rest; an audit trail records who did what; backups are encrypted. If a security incident affects your data we will notify you without undue delay and notify the marketplaces and authorities as their rules and the law require.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you or your buyers live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the LGPD in Brazil or state privacy laws in the United States), people may have the right to access, correct, delete or port their data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Write to {{EMAIL}}; we answer within the legal deadline (15 days under the LGPD, one month under the GDPR). Buyers of your store may contact you or us; we will help you answer. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

7. International transfers

Your instance runs in the region we agree with you. When data crosses borders (for example to a subprocessor), we rely on the safeguards the applicable law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

8. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and notify active clients before material changes take effect.
