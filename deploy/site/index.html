Your marketplace operation, watched around the clock. Every decision stays yours.

{{MARCA}} is an operations assistant for sellers on Amazon and Mercado Livre in the United States, Europe and Latin America. It reads your orders, listings and stock, checks margins and compliance, and tells you what needs attention. Nothing that costs money or speaks for your store happens without your approval.

Currently in closed beta, by invitation.

How it works

  1. Connect your seller account through the marketplace's own authorization page. We never ask for your marketplace password.
  2. We read what you authorize: orders, listings, prices and stock. On Amazon the service is read-only and stores no buyer name, address, e-mail or phone.
  3. You see what matters: low stock, failed synchronizations, stale data, orders stuck in a problem state, and the margin of each sale after fees and taxes.
  4. You approve every action: purchase orders, price changes and replies to buyers wait in a queue until you approve them.

Security and privacy

  • Each client runs in an isolated instance with its own database and encryption keys.
  • HTTPS everywhere; login with a password and an authenticator code; accounts lock after repeated failures.
  • Marketplace credentials are encrypted at rest and never written to logs.
  • Audit trail of every sign-in and action; encrypted backups.
  • Amazon data is deleted within 30 days when you revoke access.
